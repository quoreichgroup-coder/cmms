## Pilot order

1. Land and test the P0 access/scan fixes.
2. Verify QR on Android/iOS and NFC on an NFC-capable Android development/signed build.
3. Implemented: mobile PM, meter and downtime summaries reuse existing endpoints and themed states. Next, verify Expo presentation and access combinations, then integrate inspection/history only where source records support a truthful view.
4. Review tenant/site guards across related routes, then design a truthful asset timeline.

Scope boundaries: visual/interaction changes may preserve the current API. Adding offline queues, tenant uniqueness migrations, new timeline events, new KPI definitions or changed authorization policy requires separate engineering review and explicit acceptance criteria.
